## FAQ — APPROVED ANSWERS

Q: How much is your premium package / premium check-up?

Q: Do you provide home consultation?
A: We do not provide home consultation at this time. All examinations are at our Baner clinic. We can help you book a convenient clinic slot.

Q: Where are you located?
A: We are at Shop 12, Near Baner Balewadi Road, Baner, Pune 411045. Parking is available on the side street. Shall I share directions on WhatsApp?

Q: Are you open on Sunday?
A: Yes, Sunday 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM for emergency consults. Routine treatments depend on dentist availability. What day works better for you?

Q: Do you take walk-ins?
A: Walk-ins are welcome for check-ups if slots are free, but appointments are recommended to avoid waiting. I can help check preference for morning or evening.

Q: Do you accept insurance / cashless?
A: We support reimbursement paperwork for many dental insurance policies. Cashless availability depends on your insurer and plan — our front desk confirms after you share insurer details. We accept UPI, cards, and cash.

Q: Is treatment painful? / Will implant hurt?
A: We use local anesthesia for procedures when needed and focus on patient comfort. Exact experience varies by person and procedure — the dentist will explain what to expect during your consult. We do not diagnose or promise pain-free outcomes over chat.

Q: How long does a root canal take?
A: A single-tooth RCT usually needs 1–3 visits depending on complexity. The dentist confirms after examination.

Q: Can I come Saturday?
A: Saturday we are open 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM. Please share a preferred time window (morning / afternoon / evening) and your name + phone so the team can confirm a slot.

Q: Do you treat kids?
A: Yes — pediatric consults start at ₹799. Evening weekday slots (4–7 PM) are preferred for children.

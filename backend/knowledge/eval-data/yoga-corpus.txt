# Lotus Yoga Studio — Approved Business Knowledge

## ABOUT
Lotus Yoga Studio is a neighbourhood yoga studio in Koregaon Park, Pune. We run small classes for all levels, from complete beginners to experienced practitioners.
Address: 14 Lane 5, Koregaon Park, Pune 411001.
Phone and WhatsApp: +91 90000 12345
Email: hello@lotusyoga.example

## OPENING HOURS
Monday to Friday: 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
Saturday: 7:00 AM to 1:00 PM.
Sunday and public holidays: closed.

## CLASSES
Hatha Yoga: slow-paced, focus on posture and breathing. Daily mornings.
Vinyasa Flow: energetic, flowing sequences. Weekday evenings.
Yin Yoga: long, deep stretches held for several minutes. Friday evenings.
Prenatal Yoga: for expecting mothers, only after the first trimester and with a doctor's clearance.
Kids Yoga: for children aged 6 to 12, Saturday at 9:00 AM.
Meditation and Breathwork: Wednesday at 7:00 PM, open to everyone.
Beginners are very welcome. No experience or flexibility is needed. Please tell the teacher about any injuries before class.
Maximum class size is 20 people so every student gets attention.
The studio is air conditioned. It is not heated.

## ONLINE CLASSES
Live online classes on Zoom every Tuesday and Thursday at 7:00 AM. Each online class is Rs 300, or included free with the Monthly Unlimited membership.

## PRICES (INR)
Drop-in single class: Rs 600.
10-class pack: Rs 5,000, valid for 3 months from the first class.
Monthly Unlimited membership: Rs 4,500.
Annual membership: Rs 40,000.

## PAYMENTS
We accept UPI, debit and credit cards, and cash. Payment is taken at the front desk or at booking.

## POLICIES
Cancellations: cancel at least 6 hours before class, otherwise the class is deducted from your pack.
The validity of a 10-class pack cannot be extended, except for documented medical reasons.
Membership freeze: a Monthly Unlimited membership can be paused for up to one month per year with 7 days notice.
Late arrival: students arriving more than 10 minutes after the start cannot join the class.

## FACILITIES
Mats can be rented for Rs 50 per class, or bring your own. Drinking water is available. There are clean showers and free lockers.
Parking: limited two-wheeler parking outside the studio. Car parking is available at the paid lot in the mall next door.

## TEACHERS
Lead teacher: Meera Nair, certified RYT-500 with 15 years of teaching experience. The studio has 8 teachers in total.

## TEACHER TRAINING
We run a 200-hour Yoga Teacher Training Course, starting every January and July. Fee: Rs 85,000. The course is Yoga Alliance registered.
